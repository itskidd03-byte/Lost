# Decision log

Append-only. Every creative, technical or scope decision that changes an approved spec is recorded here.

## D-0001 — Scope baseline: no combat, no multiplayer

- Date: 2026-11-18T19:56:28.187Z
- Owner: Studio Director
- Status: accepted

**Decision.** Iron Veins ships as a single-player first-person survival mining game on WebGL and desktop. Firearms, combat systems, multiplayer/co-op, consoles, VR, mobile, mod support, voiced dialogue, surface base-building, a procedural surface world and live-service monetisation are out of scope. D23 Shooter Systems and D21 Multiplayer Engineering stay inactive for this project.

**Alternatives considered.** Add co-op (needs D21, D36 and a server budget); add firearms (needs D23, D10 weapon art, D13 weapon VFX and rebalances every hazard); ship mobile (needs low-end profiles and touch UX).

**Consequences.** Hazard simulation and economy carry the tension instead of combat. D23 and D21 tasks are not planned. Any later addition needs a new decision record and a re-plan.
