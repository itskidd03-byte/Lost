# God's Eye — Art Direction Bible

## Deliverable

### Reference and intent

Three references, each with what to borrow and what to avoid. Reference images or descriptions must be licensed or explicitly described as reference only.

### Palette

Primary, secondary and accent colours as hex values, with where each is allowed to appear.

### Shape and silhouette language

Rules a modeller or sprite artist can follow without asking: proportions, edge treatment, detail density.

### Materials and surfaces

The material families in play, roughness and metallic ranges, and what must never look plastic.

### Lighting and mood

Per area or time of day: key direction, contrast ratio, colour temperature, volumetric use.
