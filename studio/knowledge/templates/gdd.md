### Vision and pillars

The one-sentence hook, then the three pillars every system is judged against.

### Core loop

The cycle the player repeats, with a stated time to first reward and the failure state that makes it tense.

1. Observe →
2. Decide →
3. Act →
4. Feedback →
5. Reward or setback →
