Project Johan

GOD'S EYE

One immortal mind builds an infinite world, and you wear his body. The table in his cabin is not a map — it is the world itself, and every change to it becomes real, walkable ground.

A world already waits

Johan sleeps until a world is begun. After that he never stops.

In the cabin

  • WASD walk · Mouse look
  • E the table, the journal, the bed
  • On the table: drag to rotate, wheel to zoom, click a tile
  • Enter or the button to descend into the land

In the world

  • WASD walk · Shift run
  • E speak to an inhabitant
  • R return to the cabin
  • J journal · G God's Eye · Space pause

Johan's mind keeps working while you walk. The world keeps growing while you are gone.
